IN DEVELOPMENTPROJECT 01

3 kg Autonomous
Sumo Robot

A two-wheel autonomous robot designed to find an opponent, avoid the arena edge, and push the other robot out of the ring.

The design combines a front wedge with camera-based opponent detection and a sensor layer for close-range distance and boundary awareness. The platform is being developed around an Arduino Uno Q, with separate 12 V and 5 V power regulation.

Arduino Uno QComputer visionVL53L1XIBT-2Motor control
Technical breakdown ＋
  • SensingFour VL53L1X time-of-flight sensors and four IR edge-detection sensors.
  • DriveTwo-wheel drive with dual IBT-2 motor drivers.
  • VisionUSB webcam and camera-based computer vision for opponent detection.
  • PowerSeparate 12 V and 5 V regulation for the robot’s power distribution.
  • BehaviourAutonomous opponent search and attack, with boundary detection and avoidance around the arena’s white line.

Current status: development. This page describes the design direction and components; it does not present the robot as a finished product.

PROTOTYPE02

Automotive Wheel RPM & Speed Monitor

ESP32-based measurement of a real vehicle wheel using optical pulses, with wireless readings and a compact display.

ESP32ESP-NOWU8g2
Technical details ＋

A laser and LDR detect wheel pulses. Interrupt-based counting feeds RPM calculations, then wheel circumference is used to estimate vehicle speed. An OLED driven by U8g2 presents the readings; ESP-NOW carries data wirelessly.
